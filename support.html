Common Questions

  • Scanning a receipt: Tap Quick Split or open a trip and tap Scan / Upload Receipt. Point the camera at the receipt, keep it flat and well lit, and tap the shutter — or upload a photo from your library. You can also enter items by hand.
  • Splitting costs: Add the people sharing the bill, assign each item to whoever had it, and choose who paid. SquareUp calculates who owes whom and the simplest way to settle up.
  • Managing your subscription: Pro subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. To view, change, or cancel, open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions.
  • Restoring a purchase: If you reinstall the app or switch devices, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Deleting your account: Tap the profile icon on the home screen and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and any trips where you are the only member.
